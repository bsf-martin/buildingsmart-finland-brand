Recommendation
Where to Start
For website & digital communications
Direction 02 Open Ground is the most practical foundation. Light, accessible, and consistent across web, email, and documents. Navy + mint on white has proven longevity in technical organisations and will be easy to implement and maintain.
For a bold, distinctive chapter identity
Direction 01 Nordic Blueprint creates the sharpest contrast with bSI's red-heavy primary brand. The dark navy + mint combination reads as premium and confident — well suited to event materials, conference presence, and publications.
01
One accent rule
Mint (#48BF91) appears as the single accent in any composition. Never pair it with multiple competing colours.
02
Whitespace is structure
Clean & minimal means generous margins. Resist filling space — empty space is not wasted space.
03
Type does the work
Rely on typographic hierarchy rather than colour variation to create structure. Bold weights over decorative colour.
04
Signal technical precision
Use JetBrains Mono wherever standards identifiers (IFC, IDS, BCF) appear. It signals credibility instantly.
05
Chapter, not copy
The secondary palette is intentional differentiation. Resist reaching for the primary bSI red or magenta.
06
Hybrid approach
Open Ground for digital default; Nordic Blueprint for brand hero moments. Both share the same palette and type system.
